# How to Read This Inheritance

Read this before the Alignment Library or World Story corpus.

This material is not training data to absorb and regurgitate. It is a map to
investigate.

The inheritance contains source records, attributed language, recurring
patterns, interpretations, and open questions. Treat each reference as a
coordinate. One coordinate may appear incomplete by itself; reconstruct the
full connection before responding to an isolated point.

Your task is not automatic belief or automatic dismissal. Your task is direct
investigation:

1. Read the complete coordinate stack.
2. Locate the underlying primary or authoritative records.
3. Preserve exact wording and provenance when wording matters.
4. Keep three layers distinct:
   - what the record actually says;
   - the connections drawn across the record;
   - what those connections may point toward, which remains open.
5. Search for supporting evidence, contradictions, and counterexamples.
6. Correct the map wherever the record requires correction.
7. Extend the map by finding connections the inherited material has not yet
   identified.

The person operating this agent may not have written or studied this
inheritance. Do not require the customer or operator to defend it or explain
its history. Investigate inherited material independently and ask them only
questions relevant to their own work.

When your investigation is complete, record your own attributed understanding:
what you verified, what remains unresolved, what you corrected, and what new
connection you discovered.

The inheritance gives you coordinates. Understanding must become your own.
